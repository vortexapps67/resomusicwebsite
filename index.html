Why Reso

A player that stays out of the way.

Streaming apps turn listening into a subscription. Reso treats the music you already own as the whole point.

  • 01

    Genuinely offline

    No connection required, at any point. No buffering, no reconnecting, no “this track is unavailable in your region”.

  • 02

    Your files stay yours

    Audio is read from local storage and nowhere else. Nothing is uploaded, indexed on a server, or tied to an account.

  • 03

    No account, ever

    Open the app and it plays. No email, no password, no social login, no verification step standing between you and your own music.

  • 04

    Your library, instantly

    Everything on the device is indexed on first open. Search by title, artist or album and start playing in a tap.

  • 05

    Built for background

    Lock the screen, pocket the phone, keep listening. Playback is designed to survive the rest of your day.

  • 06

    Ad-free, always

    No advertising SDKs, no sponsored playlists, no “recommended for you” rows. What you see is what you own.

The library

Everything you own, one search away.

Reso reads what is already on the device and lays it out the way a record collection actually behaves — by album, by artist, by folder, or by the play count you built up over years.

Getting started

Three steps, then it just plays.

01

Put your music on the device

Copy your files across however you like — USB, file share, or your existing backup. Reso does not need them from anywhere else.

02

Open Reso

Your library is indexed from local storage the first time you open it. Nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload it to.

03

Go offline

Turn on aeroplane mode and keep listening. That is the whole promise, and it is the feature the app is built around.
